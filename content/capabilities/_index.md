intro: "Core experimental and development capabilities supporting iCORE research."
---

The iCORE laboratory integrates theory, software, and hardware through a development pipeline that supports design, simulation, prototyping, and experimental validation.

## Experimental Platforms

- Ground, aerial, and marine robotic platforms
- Mobile robot arms (KUKA & Universal Robots)
- Real-time control hardware and embedded compute nodes
- Kinematic motion capture (Optitrack)

## Control and Autonomy Development

- Model-based and data-driven controller design
- Simulation-in-the-loop and hardware-in-the-loop validation
- Embedded control design and implementation
- Optical and Electronic sensors and signal conditioning
- Telemanipulation and shared control paradigms
- Robotic perception (e.g. Simultaneous Localization and Mapping)

## Prototyping and Integration

- Mechanical & electrical system design and prototyping (CAD/CAE/Rapid Prototype)
- Rapid software integration for robotic subsystems
- Sensor calibration and system identification workflows
- Design of repeatable test procedures for comparative experiments

## Modeling and Validation

- Kinematic and dynamic analysis
- System identification
- Uncertainty quantification
- Finite Element Analysis
- Computational Fluid Dynamics

## Collaboration and Translation

- Joint academic and industry project execution
- Reproducible experiment packaging for publication
- Applied development pathways for field deployment
