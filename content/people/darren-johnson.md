Darren joined the lab in Fall 2025. His work includes: leading the project of testing new forms of intelligent PID controllers on UAVs, creating and configuring autonomous mission waypoint systems for UAVs, and implementing and testing EDMDc on quadcopter UAVs for trajectory prediction and capture. He received the S & B Engineers and Constructors Scholarship and the NASA LaSPACE GPS scholarship in Spring 2026.
